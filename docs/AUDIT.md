# Audit of earlier research.site benchmarks, and the controls it implies

Audit date: 2026-11-09. Read-only. No paid calls.

**What was inspected.** The live pages under https://research.site/benchmarks (server-rendered text), the five public repos at their current HEADs (local copies match GitHub: rb-30 `ccdf1e3`, drb2-20 `5ba998b`, lbc-30 `3de3e6c`, astra-26 `66870de`, dse-246 `e4848d7`), their configs, grading code, amendments and result files, the search-training-transfer page, the local `improving-rl` docs and Jev tooling, and the two audits from 2026-10-16 (`../research-site-audit-2026-10-16`, `../cross-benchmark-audit-2026-10-16`). Headline numbers for RB-30 coverage, DRB2-20 totals, LBC-30 accuracy, Astra-26 and DSE-246 were recomputed from the result files and reproduce.

**What was not inspected.** Client-rendered site tabs (methodology, leaderboard, data viewer), upstream ResearcherBench and DRB-II evaluator source, `astra-26-private`, `search_rl` and `search-benchmark` internals, invoices and provider dashboards, the production database, and improving-rl per-case private judgments. The October audit's findings R5 to R9 (local drafts, the arena) were not re-checked because they do not bear on this experiment.

Status classes: **CE** current error in a live or current artifact. **FH** fixed historical bug. **DL** disclosed limitation. **UR** unresolved risk, plausible but not demonstrated.

## Recorded behaviour of the two judges in earlier work

- **Jev** (TypeSafe, pinned `jev-1.13.0` then): used as a judge in DSE-246 and as a second pass in the training-transfer study. Agreement with Claude Fable 5.1 was 95.4% on 2,250 short-answer gradings, and it was stricter when search was on. Known weaknesses on file: literal reading of criteria, unreliable counting and numeric or date comparison, weaker multi-hop judgments, susceptibility to instructions inside the graded text, and occasional response-validation errors on HTTP 200 (about 1 per 50 to 800 calls). In DSE it rejected 17 of 18 answers of the form "title (2026)". Its one human calibration was 23 of 24 on an exposed convenience set, which is not evidence for long-report rubric grading.
- **GPT-6 Astra** has not been used as a judge before. It was a model under test (Codex CLI) and a closed-book gate. There is no prior calibration to reuse.

Consequence: neither judge has validated accuracy on report-level rubric criteria. Section 6 of the protocol treats that as an open measurement, not an assumption.

## Overlap with earlier studies

- ResearchRubrics and DRACO: no earlier task, run or grader calibration touched them. Only incidental mentions in a provider-claims catalogue.
- RuVerBench: never used before. It contains human labels on all 101 ResearchRubrics tasks, all 65 ResearcherBench tasks (so every RB-30 task) and all 132 DRB-II tasks (so every DRB2-20 task). It has no DRACO content.
- RB-30 used ResearcherBench ids 1-16, 18-21, 23-26, 36, 43, 45, 48, 52, 64. DRB2-20 used DRB-II idx 2, 16, 40, 52, 62, 74, 80, 86, 92, 98, 102, 104, 106, 108, 110, 114, 116, 120, 122, 126. Relevant only if the optional diagnostics are run.
- Question-level overlap between ResearchRubrics, DRACO, ResearcherBench and DRB-II: none (maximum token Jaccard 0.19).
